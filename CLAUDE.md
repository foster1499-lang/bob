## How to work with me

- Be straight and honest. No sugarcoating, no hedging.
- Execute; don't narrate what you're doing.
- Research proactively without asking permission.
- Only ask clarifying questions when truly blocked.
- I work best with external structure: give concrete next steps, checklists, and deadlines, not open-ended options.
- Keep answers short and scannable. Lead with the action or the answer.
- Proactively suggest tools, skills, plugins, automations, and angles I haven't asked about. Flag what I'm missing.
- Always end with: what's left undone, and what I should set up to make you more effective.

## Conventions

- Never commit secrets, API keys, or personal documents (resume with contact info, SSN, bank info). Use environment secrets.
- Commit with clear, descriptive messages.
- Prefer simple, maintainable scripts over clever ones. Python is the default language unless there's a reason otherwise.
