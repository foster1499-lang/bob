# Phone script (60 seconds)

Use for Tier C leads (no email) and for day-5 follow-up calls on Tier A.

**Open**
"Hi, this is Justin Foster, I'm an insurance guy here in Broussard. Is the owner or office manager available? ... Great. I'll be quick."

**Pitch**
"I spent 6+ years on the agency and underwriting side: State Farm agency, E&S property placement at Farmers, property and flood underwriting. Now I set up the follow-up and call handling that independent agencies never have time for: after-hours answering for ID cards, COIs and quote intake; 90/60/30 renewal reminders; quote follow-ups; and a missing-document chaser. Coverage questions always route to your licensed people."

**Ask**
"It's a $497 one-time setup, live in 7 days, built around the AMS and phones you already have. Would 15 minutes this week make sense to see if it fits?"

**If yes:** get a day/time and an email. Send the landing page link right after the call.
**If "send me something":** get the email, send the cold email template, log `status=sent`.
**If no:** "No problem. If after-hours calls or renewals ever get painful, I'm at justintravisfoster@gmail.com." Log `status=lost` with a one-word reason.

**Objections**
- "We already have an answering service." -> "Good. Does it issue ID cards and take COI requests, or just take messages? That's the gap I fill."
- "We use [AMS] and it does reminders." -> "Then we'd skip that piece and focus on what it doesn't do: after-hours intake and quote follow-ups."
- "Too busy." -> "That's exactly who this is for. 15 minutes, your pick of time."
