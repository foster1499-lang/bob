# Service Desk & After-Hours Script Pack
### For independent P&C agencies. By Bayou Agency Systems.

Scripts and intake forms for the calls and emails your team handles every day: ID cards, COIs, payments, changes, and claims. Use them for staff training, an answering service, or an AI receptionist.

> Service-communication templates, not coverage advice. Anything about coverage, eligibility, or "am I covered" goes to a licensed person. Review with your principal and E&O carrier before use.

---

## The one rule

**Routine request:** handle it or log it.
**Coverage question:** take a message, route to a licensed agent, promise a callback time.

Never say "you're covered," "you're not covered," or "that should be fine."

---

## Greeting

Business hours: "Thank you for calling {{agency_name}}, this is {{name}}. How can I help you?"

After hours: "Thank you for calling {{agency_name}}. Our office is closed, but I can help with ID cards, certificates, payments, and messages. If you need to report a claim, I'll give you your carrier's 24-hour claim line. How can I help?"

---

## ID card request

1. "What's the name on the policy?" 2. "Date of birth or policy number to verify?" 3. "Which vehicle?" 4. "Best email or phone to send it to?"
5. "I'll send that within {{timeframe}}." Log: name, vehicle, sent to, time.

## Certificate of insurance (COI) intake form

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Insured name | As on policy |
| Policy type(s) | GL, auto, WC, umbrella |
| Certificate holder name and address | Exact legal name |
| Additional insured requested? | Y/N. If yes, route to licensed staff. |
| Waiver of subrogation requested? | Y/N. If yes, route to licensed staff. |
| Job or project description | |
| Needed by | Date and time |
| Send to | Email |

Script: "I'll get that request to our team. Standard certificates usually go out within {{timeframe}}. If the holder needs additional insured or waiver wording, a licensed agent will confirm that with you."

## Payment questions

"I can't take card numbers over the phone, but I can text or email you the secure payment link for {{carrier}}." or "Your payment is due {{date}}. Here's how to pay: {{method}}."

If they mention a cancellation or non-pay notice: "I'm flagging this as urgent for an agent first thing {{next_business_day}}. What's the best number to reach you?"

## Policy change requests (driver, vehicle, address, mortgagee)

Collect: policy name, change requested, effective date, details (VIN, driver's license, new address, lender clause). Say: "I'll get this to a licensed agent to process. Changes aren't in effect until you get confirmation from us."

## Claims

"I'm sorry that happened. Is everyone safe?" If emergency: "Please call 911 first."
"The fastest way to report a claim is directly with {{carrier}} at {{claim_line}}, open 24 hours. I'll also let your agent know first thing tomorrow so they can follow up with you."
Log: name, policy, date of loss, short description, callback number.

## Coverage questions (route, don't answer)

"That's a great question for one of our licensed agents. I don't want to guess on coverage. Can I have {{agent}} call you by {{time}}? What's the best number?"

## Quote requests (new business)

Collect: name, phone, email, what they want quoted (home/auto/flood/business), current carrier, renewal date, best time to call. "An agent will call you by {{time}} to get your quote started."

## Voicemail greeting (after hours)

"You've reached {{agency_name}}. Our office is open {{hours}}. To report a claim, call your insurance company's 24-hour claim line on your ID card or policy. For ID cards or certificates, email {{service_email}}. Otherwise, leave your name, number, and what you need, and we'll call you back the next business day."

Date/time · Caller · Policy/insured · Request type · Details · Routed to · Callback promised by · Done (Y/N)
