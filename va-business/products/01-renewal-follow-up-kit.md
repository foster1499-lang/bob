# Renewal Follow-Up Kit
### For independent P&C agencies. By Bayou Agency Systems.

Copy, paste, and adjust to your agency's voice. Built for personal lines (home, auto, flood), works for small commercial with light edits.

> These are service-communication templates, not coverage advice. Review them with your agency principal and E&O carrier, follow your state's rules, and get written consent before sending texts (TCPA).

---

## The schedule

| When | Channel | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 90 days out | Email | Heads-up. Ask if anything changed. |
| 60 days out | Email + text | Collect changes. Offer a review. |
| 30 days out | Email + call | Present the renewal. Flag premium changes. |
| 14 days out | Text | Confirm they saw it. |
| 7 days out | Call | Last check before the policy renews. |
| Renewal day | Email | Thank them. Confirm documents. |
| 3 days after | Task | Confirm payment posted. |

## Email: 90 days out

**Subject:** Your {{policy_type}} renews on {{renewal_date}}

Hi {{first_name}},

Your {{policy_type}} policy renews on {{renewal_date}}. We'll start reviewing it soon, and it helps to know if anything changed this year:

- Remodel, new roof, or addition
- New driver, new vehicle, or one you sold
- Pool, trampoline, dog, or home business
- Change in mortgage company

Just reply to this email with anything new. If nothing changed, no need to reply.

{{agent_name}}
{{agency_name}} · {{agency_phone}}

## Email: 60 days out

**Subject:** Quick check before your renewal

Hi {{first_name}},

We're 60 days from your {{policy_type}} renewal on {{renewal_date}}. If you'd like us to review your coverage or shop other carriers, now is the best time. Reply "review" and we'll take care of it.

{{agent_name}}

## Text: 60 days out
Hi {{first_name}}, it's {{agent_name}} at {{agency_name}}. Your {{policy_type}} renews {{renewal_date}}. Anything change this year? Reply here or call {{agency_phone}}. Reply STOP to opt out.

## Email: 30 days out (no premium change)

**Subject:** Your renewal is ready

Hi {{first_name}},

Your {{policy_type}} renewal is ready. Premium: {{new_premium}} (last year {{old_premium}}). No changes to your coverage.

Your renewal documents are attached. Payment is due by {{due_date}} through {{payment_method}}.

Questions? Call {{agency_phone}}.

## Email: 30 days out (premium went up)

**Subject:** Your renewal, and what changed

Hi {{first_name}},

Your {{policy_type}} renewal came in at {{new_premium}}, up from {{old_premium}}. Here's why: {{reason_short}}.

We can review options with you. Some clients adjust a deductible or bundle policies; others prefer to keep coverage the same. A licensed agent will walk through it with you before anything changes.

Reply or call {{agency_phone}} to set up 10 minutes.

## Text: 14 days out

## Call script: 7 days out
"Hi {{first_name}}, it's {{agent_name}} with {{agency_name}}. Calling about your {{policy_type}} renewing on {{renewal_date}}. Did you have a chance to look at it? ... Any questions about the premium or coverage? [If coverage questions, licensed agent handles.] ... Great. Payment's due {{due_date}}. Anything else I can help with?"

Voicemail: "Hi {{first_name}}, {{agent_name}} with {{agency_name}}. Just making sure you saw your renewal for {{renewal_date}}. Call me back at {{agency_phone}}. Thanks."

## Email: renewal day

**Subject:** You're renewed. Thank you.

Hi {{first_name}},

Your {{policy_type}} renewed today. Thank you for staying with us. Your updated ID cards / declarations are attached.

If you ever know someone who needs help with insurance, we'd appreciate the introduction.

{{agent_name}}

## No-response rules

- **No reply by 30 days:** call. Leave a voicemail. Log it.
- **No reply by 7 days:** second call, plus text if they've opted in.
- **Premium up 15%+ and no contact:** escalate to the producer or principal.
- **Non-pay notice received:** call same day. Log the date and the conversation.
