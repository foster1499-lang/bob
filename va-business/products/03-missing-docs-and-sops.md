# Missing-Document Chaser & Core Agency SOPs
### For independent P&C agencies. By Bayou Agency Systems.

Follow-up sequences for the paperwork that stalls new business and endorsements, plus four written SOPs your team can follow without asking.

> Operational templates, not legal or coverage advice. Adjust to your carriers' requirements and your agency's procedures. Get text-message consent before texting clients (TCPA).

---

## Part 1: Missing-document chaser

Use for: signed applications, inspection access, photos, prior declarations page, proof of prior insurance, driver's license copies, elevation certificate, roof age documentation, signed no-flood or UM rejection forms.

**Day 0, email.**
Subject: One thing needed to finish your {{policy_type}}
Hi {{first_name}}, to finish your {{policy_type}} with {{carrier}}, we still need: **{{document}}**. You can {{how_to_send}}. The carrier needs it by {{deadline}}. Thanks, {{agent_name}}

**Day 2, text (if opted in).**
Hi {{first_name}}, {{agent_name}} at {{agency_name}}. Still need your {{document}} by {{deadline}}. You can reply with a photo or email {{service_email}}. Reply STOP to opt out.

**Day 4, call.** "Calling about the {{document}} for your {{policy_type}}. Do you have it handy? I can walk you through sending it."

**Day 6, final email.**
Subject: Deadline {{deadline}}: {{document}}
Hi {{first_name}}, {{carrier}} needs your {{document}} by {{deadline}}. Without it, they may {{consequence_from_carrier}}. If something's in the way, call me at {{agency_phone}} and we'll sort it out.

**Escalation.** Deadline within 48 hours and still missing: notify producer. Log every attempt with date and channel.

## Part 2: SOPs

### SOP 1: Issuing a standard certificate of insurance
**Owner:** {{CSR}} · **Backup:** {{name}} · **Target:** {{timeframe}}
1. Receive request via form, email, or phone. Use the COI intake fields.
2. Verify the policy is in force and the requester is the insured or authorized.
3. If additional insured, waiver, primary/non-contributory, or special wording is requested: STOP. Route to licensed staff.
4. Generate the certificate in {{AMS}}. Check holder name and address against the request.
5. Send to the requester and holder. Attach to the client record.
6. Log date, holder, and sender.

### SOP 2: Processing a policy change (endorsement)
**Owner:** licensed staff · **Target:** same day request is complete
1. Collect change details and effective date (see change intake).
2. Confirm all required info is present; if not, start the missing-document chaser.
3. Submit to carrier per carrier procedure. Note the confirmation number.
4. Tell the client the change is pending until carrier confirmation. Do not say it's done.
5. When confirmed, send updated documents and ID cards. Note premium change, if any.
6. Diary a 10-day follow-up if confirmation hasn't arrived.

### SOP 3: New business onboarding
1. Bound: send welcome email with policy number, carrier, claim line, and how to pay.
2. Send ID cards or declarations as soon as available.
3. Start missing-document chaser for anything outstanding.
4. Add renewal date to the renewal schedule (Renewal Follow-Up Kit).
5. Day 30: check-in email. "Any questions about your new policy?"
6. Ask for a review or referral after the first positive interaction.

### SOP 4: After-hours message handling
1. Every after-hours message is logged with time, caller, request, and callback promised.
2. Claims are directed to the carrier's 24-hour line. The agency follows up next business day.
3. First person in each morning reviews the log before anything else.
4. Callbacks: urgent (cancellations, claims, non-pay) by 10am. Routine by end of day.
5. Close each log entry with what was done and by whom.
